# Data terms and reuse conditions

This document distinguishes the software license from the reuse terms for the datasets. The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies to the software code; it does not automatically apply to data files.

## Dataset A — the author's laboratory results and diagnostic outputs

Dataset A consists of the author's own laboratory experimental results and associated diagnostic hardware/software outputs, supplied for inspection and reproducibility of the accompanying workflows. These are experimental records, not synthetic data and not a third-party dataset. The files are retained as supplied; their numerical values are not replaced with publication-derived values.

No separate data license is currently declared for Dataset A. This is a statement about the terms explicitly specified in the repository, not a claim that the data are third-party, that permission is missing, or that the author lacks authority over them. The author may specify a separate reuse license or other terms for these data. Until such terms are stated, questions about reuse beyond the purposes described in the repository should be directed to the author: alipour_r@ymail.com.

Dataset A is a limited set of laboratory measurements and diagnostic-software outputs, not a complete archive of original IR-T1 discharge shots.

## Dataset B — publication-derived values and digitizations

Dataset B contains selected numerical values transcribed from or digitized/reconstructed from the cited publications. The source publications remain subject to their respective copyright and reuse terms. Dataset B is documented with source manifests and field-level provenance; the repository does not claim ownership of the underlying publications or grant rights to reproduce their figures or text. Cite the relevant source publication when using publication-derived values.

## Synthetic inputs

Synthetic inputs are generated by the repository's code for software tests and controlled demonstrations. They are not measurements. Their role and terms do not extend to Dataset A, Dataset B, or source publications.
