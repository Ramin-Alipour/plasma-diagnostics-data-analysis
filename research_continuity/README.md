# Scientific Continuity & Research Portfolio

This layer makes the project's scientific continuity explicit without presenting synthetic reconstructions as recovered experiments.

## Research trajectory

**2006–2013 — Physics and experimental foundation**  
BSc/MSc physics training and experimental/data-analysis foundation.

**2013–2017 — IR-T1 experimental plasma research**  
Experimental plasma diagnostics and analysis spanning Doppler spectroscopy, hard X-ray measurements, electrostatic probes, magnetic fluctuations/MHD, and transport-related analysis.

**2017–2018 — Independent research**  
Continuation of plasma-physics analysis and research activity after the primary IR-T1 appointment.

**2018–2026 — Independent scientific & technical work**  
Scientific/technical consulting and independent analysis using Python/MATLAB-oriented workflows, signal processing, quantitative data analysis, and materials-characterization data analysis. Project-specific client datasets are not distributed.

**2026 — Publication-grounded reproducible reconstruction**  
A public, verifiable computational layer that uses six peer-reviewed IR-T1 publications as quantitative constraints and runs reconstructed synthetic measurements through reusable diagnostic-analysis workflows.

## What can be verified here?

The repository provides executable evidence for current computational capabilities: signal processing, spectroscopy analysis, HXR analysis, multichannel magnetic analysis, electrostatic-probe workflows, turbulence/transport calculations, uncertainty/sensitivity studies, provenance, tests, and notebook-based demonstrations.

The repository does **not** claim to contain raw IR-T1 shot data or undisclosed client data. Where post-2018 project data are unavailable for public release, the evidence is represented through reusable methodology and publication-grounded demonstrations rather than invented project artifacts.
