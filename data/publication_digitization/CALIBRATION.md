# P06 Figure 2 digitization calibration

The P06 Figure 2 panels were rendered from the publication PDF at 120 dpi and digitized from the published plot image.

- Frequency axis: 0–100 kHz, linear.
- Panel a/b amplitude axis: 0–0.5 a.u., linear.
- Panel c amplitude axis: 0–5 a.u., linear.
- One horizontal pixel corresponds to approximately 0.35 kHz in the extracted plot regions.
- One vertical pixel corresponds to approximately 0.0037 a.u. in panels a/b and 0.037 a.u. in panel c.
- The stored trace is sampled every 2 horizontal pixels to avoid false precision.
- Values are explicitly labelled `digitized_from_published_figure`; they are not raw experimental data.
- Peak frequency is obtained from a short median-smoothed envelope and is intended as a figure-level quantitative estimate, not a replacement for the paper's text-reported approximately 44 kHz value.
